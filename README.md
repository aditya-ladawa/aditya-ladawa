## Inside a build

### [PeCal Pulse](https://github.com/aditya-ladawa/pecal-pulse)

A voice-operated sales workspace built by our team of four. First place at Perschmann Calibration's Hack The Lab. Ask Pulse for a chart, open an account or prepare a follow-up draft while the forecasting and segmentation stay available to inspect.

[<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aditya-ladawa/pecal-pulse/main/media/pulse-inline-charts.jpg" width="100%" alt="PeCal Pulse workspace with agent-created charts inside its conversation panel" />](https://github.com/aditya-ladawa/pecal-pulse)

<sub>Next.js · FastAPI · scikit-learn · LangGraph · LiveKit. Public demo uses synthetic data. Historical activity prediction reached held-out ROC AUC 0.81; a simple baseline beat the more complex volume models.</sub>
